## 3. Recommended Presentation & Consulting Flow

When speaking with school boards, administrative cabinets, or conference audiences, use this sequence:

1. **The Provocation (Writing Hub):** Open with [`screen-time-wrong-question`](https://minerclass.github.io/screen-time-wrong-question/) to challenge the media panic around aggregate device hours.
2. **The Policy Evidence (Evidence Hub):** Transition to [`k12-device-policy-evidence-hub`](https://minerclass.github.io/k12-device-policy-evidence-hub/) to show empirical data (Tamim, Zheng, Dutch Ban vs. SMART Schools, Sweden) and prove hardware bans do not automatically lift student attainment.
3. **The Deep Dive (Writing & Policy Hub):** Navigate to [`pouch-and-bypass`](https://minerclass.github.io/screen-time-wrong-question/pouch-and-bypass/) to explain why pouching phones leaves the Chromebook AI bypass untouched.
4. **The Experiential Simulation (Gaming Hub):** Engage attendees with [`orality_game`](https://minerclass.github.io/orality_game/) or [`friction_game`](https://minerclass.github.io/friction_game/) to experience the feeling of cognitive bypass vs. desirable difficulty.
5. **The Practical Audit (District Leadership):** Run the interactive **6-Question District Policy Audit** and generate a printable board memo.
6. **The Pedagogical Solution (Conference & Dissertation Hubs):** Conclude with [`screen-practice-compass`](https://minerclass.github.io/screen-practice-compass/) and [`genAI-ML-pedagogy-of-friction-site`](https://minerclass.github.io/genAI-ML-pedagogy-of-friction-site/) for actionable instructional strategies.
